# ADR-0004: Period-0 Energy Ledger Schema & Ground State Minimization

- **Status**: Proposed
- **Date**: 2026-09-03
- **Author**: Phase Mirror Formal Methods Engineering & Echonomics Group
- **Decider**: Echonomics Architectural Review Board

## Context

Hundian Social Physics tracks participant role allocations and derived multiplicity $M = n_{\text{unpaired}} + 1$. While seating log transactions manage discrete occupancy states, system stability and state selection depend on total system energy. Combining pairwise friction and purpose attraction onto a single scalar masks screening effects and produces inverted ground states.

## Decision

1. **Separated Ledger Mandate**:
   - Pairwise interaction friction $V_{\text{pair}} \ge 0$ and nuclear purpose attraction $V_{\text{nuc}} \ge 0$ MUST be maintained on separate, independent ledgers ($V_{\text{pair}} \ne V_{\text{nuc}}$).

2. **Energy Function & Sign Convention**:
   - Total system energy is defined as $E = V_{\text{pair}} - V_{\text{nuc}}$.
   - $V_{\text{pair}}$ represents repulsion/friction costs incurred by pairing ($\beta$ occupants).
   - $V_{\text{nuc}}$ represents binding attraction generated by nuclear purpose alignment.

3. **Ground State Principle**:
   - Ground state is defined as the occupancy pattern that minimizes total energy $E = V_{\text{pair}} - V_{\text{nuc}}$ at a fixed headcount $N$, fixed degenerate role set $D$, and fixed period $P$.
   - Ground state is independent of token balances or raw multiplicity $M$.

## Consequences

- Prevents false energy cancellation between friction and purpose attraction.
- Machine-checked guarantee that ground state selection minimizes $E = V_{\text{pair}} - V_{\text{nuc}}$.
